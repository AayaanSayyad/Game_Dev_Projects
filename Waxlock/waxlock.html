How to Play

  • 🎮Arrow keys or WASD steer you directly. Tap or click also turns you 90°. You're always moving forward.
  • 🕯Every four tiles, you drip wax on the tile behind you. It hardens into a wall after two seconds.
  • 🕯Light every wick to clear the level. Avoid water, hardened wax, and the grid's edge.
  • 💨Wind gusts nudge you one tile sideways. Watch for the shimmering arrow — it warns you a second early.
  • 🔥Embers flare up on random tiles in later levels. A warm glow warns you just before they catch.
  • ✨Gold wicks offer a bonus: melt a wall, slow your wax, dodge a gust, or leave a drip that never hardens.
